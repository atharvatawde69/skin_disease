The ABCDE rule: how to check a mole
A. AsymmetryOne half does not match the other half.
B. BorderEdges are ragged, notched or blurred.
C. ColourSeveral shades of brown, black, red, white or blue.
D. DiameterLarger than about 6 mm (a pencil eraser), though melanomas can be smaller.
E. EvolvingChanges in size, shape or colour, or new bleeding, itching or crusting.

If a spot matches any of these, or simply worries you, have a doctor look at it. No app can replace a skin examination.
